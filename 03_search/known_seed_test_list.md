## Interpreting test results

- "Positive" means an expected sensitive search seed, not a guarantee of core inclusion. Xu (POS-S1) is intentionally positive for retrieval but an eligibility boundary.
- "Negative eligibility" and "boundary" mean the record is expected to be found and then excluded from, or tagged within, the main A/B map. Neither is a negative retrieval control. BND-W1 and the outside-window samples of POS-C4/POS-C5 are **eligible** with an outside-window tag; they are not exclusions.
- Actual discovery is specific to the database and platform and is recorded only after the test. Do not infer a query miss from absence in a database that does not index that source type or version.
- A title lookup or exact DOI/PubMed lookup may confirm that a database holds the record, but it is a separate diagnostic. Test the complete route query itself and save the exact results page or strategy history.
- Identifier verification (PubMed E-utilities `esummary`, 2026-10-04) confirms only that the PMID, DOI, title and journal match. It is not a database-retrieval test or an eligibility decision.
- Add further independently identified seeds during pilot calibration, including null/negative exercise findings and non-English indexed records. Use the saved random seed for the separate 50-record human screening pilot.
