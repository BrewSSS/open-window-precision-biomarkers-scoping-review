# V3 database-search strategy draft — DRAFT / NOT PRESS-REVIEWED / NOT EXECUTED

Version: 0.4-draft, 2026-10-04
Protocol context: protocol v3.0 (draft for team freeze, 4 October 2026); binding decisions in `01_protocol/v3_design_contract.txt` (sections 1-3 and 8.3a). The v3 contract supersedes the framing parts of `01_protocol/v2_design_contract.txt`; v2 search rules not changed by v3 carry over unchanged.
Purpose: translate the approved search logic into five platform-specific syntax drafts and a separate Embase/Ovid adaptation. These are design artifacts for vocabulary validation, known-seed testing, PRESS review, and human pilot—not executed searches. No hit counts, exports, final search date, or PRISMA counts exist.

## 1. Search logic and boundaries

Run two complementary routes in each database and combine their exports after download and deduplication:

- Route EI: **E AND I** (exercise/exertion AND direct immune cells, function, or recognized immune mediators).
- Route EO: **E AND O** (exercise/exertion AND omics/molecular-profiling terms).
- Union: **E AND (I OR O)**. Do not add an age, sex, training-status, health, human, language, publication-date, peer-review, post-exercise, biomarker, precision, infection-risk, or “open window” filter. Apply population/design/timing/immune-link rules in human screening. Reviews and animal studies may appear in searches but are not core eligible original human reports; reviews support citation chasing only. Preprints are sought in a separately logged supplemental search and kept out of peer-reviewed core counts.

**Search by construct (v3 contract §3 and ruling 8.3a).** The exercise-induced immune "open window" is the review's organising construct: it defines the post-exercise period, sampling time points and marker families to be charted. It is not searched as a phrase. The 2026-10-04 collision re-check found only 18 PubMed title/abstract records (2018 to 2026-10-04) that combine "open window"/"open-window" with exercise/athlete/sport and immune terms (`02_preliminary/collision_recheck_2026-10-04.md` §5 item 1 and §6 query a). Original studies rarely use the phrase. The construct is therefore captured by the unchanged two-route strategy E AND (I OR O). The 0-72 h organising window, the adult population, the A/B design rules and the immune-link rule are all applied at screening, not in the query. "Open window"-type terms (`"open window"`, `"open-window"`, `immunodepress*`, `"immune suppression"`, `"exercise-induced immunosuppression"`) may be used only (a) as an optional OR supplement inside the I block, so that a route reads E AND (I OR OW) and is never narrowed, or (b) through citation chasing from open-window-framed reviews. They are never AND terms, filters or eligibility criteria. If the OR supplement is used, log it as its own search-history line and record its marginal yield during seed testing. Window-based charting has a published precedent in the "prespecified recovery windows" of Trybulski et al. 2026 (Int J Mol Sci; PMID 42278474; doi:10.3390/ijms27114950). That precedent concerns charting, not searching.

A broader `post-exercise`/recovery add-on may be used only as a **diagnostic sensitivity/precision comparison** during strategy validation. It is never part of the main EI/EO query unless a documented pilot demonstrates acceptable recall and the team explicitly amends the contract. Immediate-only studies remain discoverable; during-only studies are screened out. There is no 30-min minimum. The 0-72 h post-cessation interval is an **organising window** applied at screening and charting. It is not a search limit and not an inclusion cap. Immediate (0 h) samples are kept. Studies whose only post-cessation samples fall later than 72 h stay eligible if they otherwise qualify; those samples are extracted and tagged "outside-window recovery". No new exclusion code exists for them.

Population, exposure characteristics and biology are intentionally not encoded as a search block. Screening determines adult age (>= 18 y; mixed-age cohorts only when an adult stratum is separable; < 18 y excluded under FT03); acute Core A vs repeated-bout Support B (retained as the cross-bout open-window monitoring layer) vs background; health, active infection, time since the identifiable bout and position relative to the organising window, and whether omics has a stated immune objective/identifiable immune candidates. Post-hoc pathway enrichment in general muscle/metabolic work alone is insufficient for main inclusion. Precision language, the phrase "open window", author stance on the open-window hypothesis, and clinical/functional validation are not eligibility requirements. Exercise intensity, duration and mode are recorded at extraction and used as evidence-map strata. They are never search terms that narrow retrieval and never eligibility thresholds (v3 contract 2.1); the E block's mode/intensity words (for example `HIIT`, `sprint*`, `marathon*`) are OR synonyms that widen retrieval only.

## 2. Draft concept vocabularies (to be calibrated)

**E — exercise/exertion/sport context:** `exercis*`, `physical activit*`, `physical exertion`, `sport*`, `training`, `train*`, `workout*`, `athlet*`, `competition*`, `exercise test*`, `exercise challenge*`, `physical effort`, `run`/`runn*`/`running`, `jog`/`jogg*`/`jogging`, `cycl*`/`cycling`, `bicycl*`, `marathon*`, `race*`, `resistance train*`, `strength training`, `endurance`, `aerobic`, `interval`, `HIIT`, `sprint*`, `weightlift*`, and `swim*`. Keep `train*` in the draft because it may retrieve otherwise unlabelled training studies; it may be noisy, as may `race*` and broad run/race roots. During seed/pilot review log each term’s marginal eligible yield and false-retrieval pattern before any documented change; `train*` is retained pending that test, and no deletion is an improvement without recall evidence.

**I — immune measure/link:** retain the existing broad cell/function vocabulary and explicitly add marker-name routes: `IgA`, `sIgA`, `SIgA`, `secretory IgA`, `secretory immunoglobulin A`, `immunoglobulin A`, `interleukin*`, `interleukin-6`, `IL-6`/`IL6`, `CRP`, `C-reactive protein`, `phagocyt*`, and `cytotoxic*`/`immunologic cytotoxicity`. These permit studies whose titles/abstracts name only the measured immune marker. Adjust only after seed/pilot sensitivity review; do not add cortisol/CK/lactate as immune terms.

**O — omics/molecular profiling:** `omic*`, `proteom*`, `metabolom*`, `lipidom*`, `transcriptom*`, `epigenom*`, `methylom*`, `multiomic*`, `multi-omic*`, `single cell`, `single-cell`, `scRNA-seq`, `RNA-seq`, `RNA sequencing`, `microRNA`, `miRNA`, `lncRNA`, `circRNA`, `mass spectrometry`, `protein profiling`, `metabolite profiling`, `high-throughput sequencing`. The O route is deliberately sensitive and may retrieve generic exercise omics; screen immune relevance and do not count post-hoc enrichment alone as an eligibility link.

For each platform, enter the displayed E, I and O block contents as separate search-history lines (omit the `E_PLATFORM =` label), then combine the E line with I for Route EI and with O for Route EO using that platform’s own advanced-search/history controls. Do not assume that another platform’s search-history reference syntax transfers. Save the platform-displayed combined query and its parsed translation. Export the EI and EO result sets separately before deduplication.

These are free-text drafts. Controlled vocabulary is a required pre-run addition for databases with thesauri because older records may lack abstracts. The public 2026 NLM MeSH Browser confirms candidates Exercise (D015444), Physical Exertion (D005082), Running (D012420), Immunity (D007109), Immunoglobulin A (D007070), Immunoglobulin A, Secretory (D007071), Interleukins (D007378), Interleukin-6 (D015850), C-Reactive Protein (D002097), Phagocytosis (D010587), and Cytotoxicity, Immunologic (D003602). These candidates are not yet tested in PubMed Search Details; include appropriate verified headings in the final PubMed E/I blocks before running. Also assess headings for the broader immune-cell/function/cytokine vocabulary and O-block profiling concepts; document any concept with no suitable controlled heading. For Embase.com/Ovid, map these concepts independently to Emtree; for SPORTDiscus, inspect its thesaurus and map relevant descriptors. Exact preferred headings, scope notes, narrower terms, and explode choices remain **PENDING LIVE PLATFORM THESAURUS VALIDATION** and are a formal-run gate. Do not copy MeSH labels into Emtree or EBSCO. WoS and Scopus use indexed free-text fields here and have no thesaurus line in this strategy.
