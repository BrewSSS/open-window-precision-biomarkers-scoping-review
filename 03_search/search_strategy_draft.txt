# V3 database-search strategy draft — DRAFT / NOT PRESS-REVIEWED / NOT EXECUTED

Version: 0.6-draft, 2026-10-04 (PRESS must-fix items not reserved for A applied by D; see §11)
Protocol context: protocol v3.0 (draft for team freeze, 4 October 2026); binding decisions in `01_protocol/v3_design_contract.txt` (sections 1-3 and 8.3a). The v3 contract supersedes the framing parts of `01_protocol/v2_design_contract.txt`; v2 search rules not changed by v3 carry over unchanged.
Purpose: translate the approved search logic into five platform-specific syntax drafts and a separate Embase/Ovid adaptation. These are design artifacts for vocabulary validation, known-seed testing, PRESS review, and human pilot—not executed searches. No formal hit counts, exports, final search date, or PRISMA counts exist. PubMed parser-validation counts (E-utilities, 2026-10-04) are recorded only in `platform_validation_2026-10-04.md` and the log's `parser_validation_runs` section; they are not search results.

## 1. Search logic and boundaries

Run two complementary routes in each database and combine their exports after download and deduplication:

- Route EI: **E AND I** (exercise/exertion AND direct immune cells, function, or recognized immune mediators).
- Route EO: **E AND O** (exercise/exertion AND omics/molecular-profiling terms).
- Union: **E AND (I OR O)**. Do not add an age, sex, training-status, health, human, language, publication-date, peer-review, post-exercise, biomarker, precision, infection-risk, or “open window” filter. Apply population/design/timing/immune-link rules in human screening. Reviews and animal studies may appear in searches but are not core eligible original human reports; reviews support citation chasing only. Preprints are sought in a separately logged supplemental search and kept out of peer-reviewed core counts.

**Search by construct (v3 contract §3 and ruling 8.3a).** The exercise-induced immune "open window" is the review's organising construct: it defines the post-exercise period, sampling time points and marker families to be charted. It is not searched as a phrase. The 2026-10-04 collision re-check found only 18 PubMed title/abstract records (2018 to 2026-10-04) that combine "open window"/"open-window" with exercise/athlete/sport and immune terms (`02_preliminary/collision_recheck_2026-10-04.md` §5 item 1 and §6 query a). Original studies rarely use the phrase. The construct is therefore captured by the unchanged two-route strategy E AND (I OR O). The 0-72 h organising window, the adult population, the A/B design rules and the immune-link rule are all applied at screening, not in the query. "Open window"-type terms (`"open window"`, `"open-window"`, `immunodepress*`, `"immune suppression"`, `"exercise-induced immunosuppression"`) may be used only (a) as an optional OR supplement inside the I block, so that a route reads E AND (I OR OW) and is never narrowed, or (b) through citation chasing from open-window-framed reviews. They are never AND terms, filters or eligibility criteria. If the OR supplement is used, log it as its own search-history line and record its marginal yield during seed testing. Window-based charting has a published precedent in the "prespecified recovery windows" of Trybulski et al. 2026 (Int J Mol Sci; PMID 42278474; doi:10.3390/ijms27114950). That precedent concerns charting, not searching.

A broader `post-exercise`/recovery add-on may be used only as a **diagnostic sensitivity/precision comparison** during strategy validation. It is never part of the main EI/EO query unless a documented pilot demonstrates acceptable recall and the team explicitly amends the contract. Immediate-only studies remain discoverable; during-only studies are screened out. There is no 30-min minimum. The 0-72 h post-cessation interval is an **organising window** applied at screening and charting. It is not a search limit and not an inclusion cap. Immediate (0 h) samples are kept. Studies whose only post-cessation samples fall later than 72 h stay eligible if they otherwise qualify; those samples are extracted and tagged "outside-window recovery". No new exclusion code exists for them.

Population, exposure characteristics and biology are intentionally not encoded as a search block. Screening determines adult age (>= 18 y; mixed-age cohorts only when an adult stratum is separable; < 18 y excluded under FT03); acute Core A vs repeated-bout Support B (retained as the cross-bout open-window monitoring layer) vs background; health, active infection, time since the identifiable bout and position relative to the organising window, and whether omics has a stated immune objective/identifiable immune candidates. Post-hoc pathway enrichment in general muscle/metabolic work alone is insufficient for main inclusion. Precision language, the phrase "open window", author stance on the open-window hypothesis, and clinical/functional validation are not eligibility requirements. Exercise intensity, duration and mode are recorded at extraction and used as evidence-map strata. They are never search terms that narrow retrieval and never eligibility thresholds (v3 contract 2.1); the E block's mode/intensity words (for example `HIIT`, `sprint*`, `marathon*`) are OR synonyms that widen retrieval only.

## 2. Draft concept vocabularies (to be calibrated)

**E — exercise/exertion/sport context:** `exercis*`, `physical activit*`, `physical exertion`, `sport*`, `training`, `train*`, `workout*`, `athlet*`, `competition*`, `exercise test*`, `exercise challenge*`, `physical effort`, `run`/`runn*`/`running`, `jog`/`jogg*`/`jogging`, `cycling`, `cyclist*`, `cycle ergomet*`, `ergomet*`, `bicycl*`, `marathon*`, `race*`, `resistance train*`, `strength training`, `endurance`, `aerobic`, `interval train*`, `interval exercis*`, `sprint interval*`, `high-intensity interval*`, `HIIT`, `HIIE`, `sprint*`, `weightlift*`, and `swim*`. v0.6 replaced the over-truncated `cycl*` (it also matched cyclophosphamide, cyclin, cyclic and cell cycle) and the bare word `interval` ("confidence interval") with these specific forms (PRESS 4.1/4.2); all 24 PubMed seeds were still retrieved (`platform_validation_2026-10-04.md`, Addendum 2). Keep `train*` in the draft (contract; it may retrieve otherwise unlabelled training studies); it may be noisy, as may `race*` and broad run/race roots. Known `train*` noise: "trained immunity" (innate-immune memory, unrelated to exercise training) satisfies E through `train*` and I through `immun*` at once; on 2026-10-04 all 1,896 PubMed records containing that phrase were inside the v0.6 union. They are removed at title/abstract screening, never by a NOT term. During seed/pilot review log each term’s marginal eligible yield and false-retrieval pattern before any documented change; `train*` is retained pending that test, and no deletion is an improvement without recall evidence.

**I — immune measure/link:** retain the existing broad cell/function vocabulary and explicitly add marker-name routes: `IgA`, `sIgA`, `SIgA`, `secretory IgA`, `secretory immunoglobulin A`, `immunoglobulin A`, `interleukin*`, `interleukin-6`, `IL-6`/`IL6`, `CRP`, `C-reactive protein`, `phagocyt*`, and `cytotoxic*`/`immunologic cytotoxicity`. v0.6 adds leukocyte-population terms (`white blood cell*`, `mononuclear cell*`, `PBMC*`, `granulocyt*`, `lymphopeni*`, `lymphocytopeni*`, `leukocytosis`) and cytokine names and abbreviations (`TNF*`, `tumor necrosis factor*`/`tumour necrosis factor*`, `IFN*`, `interferon*`, `IL-10`/`IL10`, `IL-1`/`IL1`/`IL-1beta`, `IL-8`/`IL8`) (PRESS 4.3/4.7). PubMed needs at least 4 characters before `*`, so its block lists `TNF`, `"TNF-alpha"`, `IFN` and `"IFN-gamma"` explicitly instead of `TNF*`/`IFN*`; PubMed normalises the Greek forms (TNF-α, IL-1β) to the same index terms. `lymphocytopeni*` and `leukocytosis` are already matched by `lymphocyt*`/`leukocyt*` and are listed for transparency. These permit studies whose titles/abstracts name only the measured immune marker. Infection/URTI wording is not an I synonym in this draft; how infection terms are handled is reserved for A (PRESS 1.1a). Adjust only after seed/pilot sensitivity review; do not add cortisol/CK/lactate as immune terms.

**O — omics/molecular profiling:** `omic*`, `proteom*`, `metabolom*`, `lipidom*`, `transcriptom*`, `epigenom*`, `epigenet*`, `DNA methylation`, `methylom*`, `multiomic*`, `multi-omic*`, `single cell`, `single-cell`, `scRNA-seq`, `RNA-seq`, `RNA sequencing`, `microRNA*`, `miRNA*`, `lncRNA*`, `circRNA*`, `mass spectrometry`, `protein profiling`, `metabolite profiling`, `high-throughput sequencing`, `microarray*`, `gene expression profil*`, `expression profil*` (v0.6 added epigenetics, DNA methylation, microarray and expression-profiling terms and truncated the ncRNA terms; PRESS 4.4-4.6). The O route is deliberately sensitive and may retrieve generic exercise omics; screen immune relevance and do not count post-hoc enrichment alone as an eligibility link.

For each platform, enter the displayed E, I and O block contents as separate search-history lines (omit the `E_PLATFORM =` label), then combine the E line with I for Route EI and with O for Route EO using that platform’s own advanced-search/history controls. Do not assume that another platform’s search-history reference syntax transfers. Save the platform-displayed combined query and its parsed translation. Export the EI and EO result sets separately before deduplication.

The E/I/O vocabularies above are free text. Controlled vocabulary is a required pre-run addition for databases with thesauri because older records may lack abstracts. **PubMed (v0.6):** E, I and O MeSH lines are now part of the PubMed draft (§3). Each of the 42 headings, and Post-Exercise Recovery (D000096062) for the diagnostic T test only, was confirmed on 2026-10-04 in E-utilities db=mesh as exactly one descriptor whose preferred heading matches the string used (UIs in `platform_validation_2026-10-04.md`, Addendum 2). All are exploded (`[Mesh]`) except Mass Spectrometry, which is `[Mesh:NoExp]`: its explosion (GC-MS, LC-MS, tandem MS and similar) adds 2,973 analytical-method records to the union beyond the NoExp form (PRESS 3.2). The 11 v0.5 candidates are kept; those now redundant under an exploded parent stay to document marker intent. The heading set remains subject to A's PRESS sign-off. **Embase (Embase.com or Ovid) and SPORTDiscus:** candidate Emtree and SPORTDiscus-thesaurus lines are given in §6 and §7. They are marked **PENDING LIVE THESAURUS VALIDATION**: the concept labels there are lookup prompts, not verified preferred terms, and D confirms each preferred term, scope note, explosion choice and date at login before any line is used. Do not copy MeSH labels into Emtree or EBSCO. Document any concept with no suitable controlled heading. Exact preferred headings, scope notes, narrower terms and explode choices on those platforms are a formal-run gate. WoS and Scopus use indexed free-text fields here and have no thesaurus line in this strategy.
