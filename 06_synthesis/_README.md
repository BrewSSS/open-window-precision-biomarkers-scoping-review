# 06 · Synthesis

This directory contains the v2 synthesis design only. No formal database search, screening, extraction, included-study count, PRISMA flow count, or synthesis result has been generated.

- [`synthesis_plan.md`](synthesis_plan.md) defines the RQ-to-output map, A/B strata, analysis units, deduplication and denominator rules, null/missingness handling, time displays, precision domains, and planned tables/maps aligned to the canonical three-map order: time by immune compartment/matrix; candidate monitoring use by seven precision domains; cohort-level validation links.
- [`evidence_map_spec.json`](evidence_map_spec.json) is the machine-readable design specification for linked cohort/report/assay units, evidence-state codes, outputs, and prohibited inferences.
- [`manuscript_blueprint.md`](manuscript_blueprint.md) gives the future Frontiers in Immunology manuscript structure and reporting plan. The method remains a JBI/PCC scoping review with PRISMA-ScR/PRISMA-S; check the live section dropdown and current instructions at submission. Targeting this venue does not imply acceptance.

The broader evidence landscape belongs in Table 1 and may annotate Figure 1; it is not a fourth analytic map. Figure 2 includes seven separate domains, with independent validation and tested use explicitly distinguished from a proposed use.

Use the actual final search date as the review cutoff. The 2026-10-02 public-web reconnaissance is preliminary and must not be described as a completed five-database search. Preserve NR, NA, UNCLEAR, and not-retrieved states separately. Count independent cohorts and publication reports separately; never turn assays, timepoints, cells, or technical replicates into independent evidence. A and B can overlap, so report the union for overall totals. For B, require the same immune-related marker in the same people after at least two identifiable bouts with recoverable timing and a baseline/comparator. Keep preprints in a separate frontier register. Do not enter results, PRISMA counts, authors, funding, registration details, or other declarations until verified from project records or confirmed by the team.
