# 06 · Synthesis

This directory contains the v3 synthesis design only (3.0 (draft for team freeze) - 4 October 2026; `01_protocol/v3_design_contract.txt`). No formal database search, screening, extraction, included-study count, PRISMA flow count, or synthesis result has been generated.

The exercise-induced immune "open window" is used solely as an organising construct that defines the 0–72 h post-exercise window, the sampling time points and the marker families to be charted; the synthesis does not adjudicate whether an open window exists. The deliverable is a per-marker validation-readiness map. **Definition (contract 8.3e), referred to by every mention below: Validation-readiness map: a per-marker, domain-by-domain descriptive profile across the seven precision domains; not a score, ranking, grade or development ladder.**

- [`synthesis_plan.md`](synthesis_plan.md) defines the RQ-to-output map, A/B strata (`A_core_acute`, `B_support_repeated_bouts`), analysis units, deduplication and denominator rules, the evidence-state mapping table, null/missingness handling, time displays with window position, precision domains, and planned tables/maps in the canonical three-map order: (1) open-window time × immune compartment/marker family; (2) per-marker validation-readiness map (marker/family × seven domains; conventional vs omics); (3) cohort-level validation links.
- [`evidence_map_spec.json`](evidence_map_spec.json) is the machine-readable design specification for linked cohort/report/assay units (count fields named as in the extraction template), the shared time-bin and scope vocabularies, evidence-state codes and crosswalk, outputs, and prohibited inferences.
- [`manuscript_blueprint.md`](manuscript_blueprint.md) gives the future Frontiers in Immunology manuscript structure and reporting plan, with the approved English and Chinese titles. The method remains a JBI/PCC scoping review with PRISMA-ScR/PRISMA-S; check the live section dropdown and current instructions at submission. Targeting this venue does not imply acceptance.

The broader evidence landscape belongs in Table 1 and may annotate Figure 1; it is not a fourth analytic map. Figure 2 includes seven separate domains, with independent validation and tested use explicitly distinguished from a proposed use; it carries no score or ranking (definition above).

Use the actual final search date as the review cutoff. The 2026-10-02 public-web reconnaissance and the 2026-10-04 collision re-check are preliminary and must not be described as a completed formal database search; neither may the superseded PubMed v0.7 export of 2026-10-05 (amendment PRE-006). Preserve NR, NA, UNCLEAR, and not-retrieved states separately. Count independent cohorts and publication reports separately; never turn assays, timepoints, cells, or technical replicates into independent evidence. A and B can overlap, so report the union for overall totals. For B, require the same immune-related marker in the same people after at least two identifiable bouts with recoverable timing and a baseline/comparator. Samples beyond 72 h are charted as outside-window recovery, not dropped. Keep preprints in a separate frontier register. Do not enter results, PRISMA counts, authors, funding, archive DOI or other declarations until verified from project records or confirmed by the team.
