# Full-text boundary exercises (before formal full-text screening)

**Rules:** protocol v3.2 (sections C, D, F; PRE-004 age rule and RETAIN_BACKGROUND; PRE-008 V layer, v1.2 element definitions), `screening_manual.md` 3C, `fulltext_exclusion_codes.json`. Required by `calibration_plan.md` ("Full-text stage"). Prepared 2026-10-08.

**Sources.** Every exercise uses a report whose full text the team already holds: the 10 charting-pilot reports in the git-ignored `05_extraction/pilot_2026-10-05/fulltexts/` folder (hashes in `fulltexts_manifest.json`; R62 via `fulltext_requests/fulltext_requests.csv`, FTR-001). Page numbers are PDF/journal pages ("p.3" = "3 of 11"). Formal record IDs are given where the report is in the formal pool. Exercises are training material. They are not formal decisions and add nothing to any count.

**Procedure.**
1. B and C each answer all 15 exercises alone, before reading Part 2. For each exercise, record the answer in the same columns as the full-text workbook: disposition, primary FT code (EXCLUDE only), age-rule check, validation element confirmed (yes/no/unclear) and a one-line reason with page.
2. D compares the two sets of answers with the key. B, C and D discuss every exercise where an answer differs from the key or from the other reviewer.
3. D logs the outcome of each discussion: the reading agreed, any manual clarification, and any question left open. Where discussion shows that the key is wrong, the key is corrected and the change is logged; the key is not defended for its own sake.

**Pass rule.** Each reviewer matches the key on the disposition (and on the FT code for EXCLUDE) in at least 12 of 15 exercises, and every discrepancy has been discussed and logged. The rule applies only to these exercises. Agreement at formal full text is reported descriptively (calibration_plan.md); this rule does not set a full-text threshold. If a reviewer falls below 12, that reviewer re-reads the relevant manual sections and redoes the exercises they got wrong; a second round is not needed.

**Workbook values.** The full-text workbook has no FRONTIER_PREPRINT or AWAITING_TRANSLATION option. The V list comes from searches limited to journal articles, so neither case should arise. If one does, enter AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION and write "frontier preprint" or "translation" in the comment; D then routes the report.

## Part 2: Key (expected disposition, why, tempting wrong answer)

**X01 · INCLUDE_A · age: adults_confirmed · E5: yes (omics_discovery).** "Concept Paper" is the journal's label; the report gives original human data (methylation array on isolated NK cells, pre and 1 min post). FT01 covers reviews, protocols, editorials and conference abstracts, not article-type labels. Any identifiable bout qualifies, so a short maximal test is fine. Mean − 2·SD = 45.4 y, so the cohort is adult; flag it as an older-adult subgroup and chart the 1-min sample as immediate-only response. *Tempting:* FT01 on the label; FT04 because "a fitness test is not exercise"; FT03 for older age.

**X02 · RETAIN_BACKGROUND (secondary failed dimension FT04) · age: adults_confirmed (range 20–40 y) · E5: yes.** The report is original, on generally healthy adults, and has an eligible immune link (PBMC single-cell omics). Its only failure is the habitual cross-sectional exposure: there is no identifiable bout, and timing relative to the last session is not recorded. Chart at reduced depth. *Tempting:* EXCLUDE FT04, which RETAIN_BACKGROUND replaces for exactly this pattern; FT06, but SED is a comparator and the failure is the missing bout, which comes first in the hierarchy; INCLUDE A, on the strength of the single-cell "precision" modality.

**X03 · AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION · age: mean_minus_2SD_below_18_unresolved · E5: yes (omics_discovery; repeated_monitoring over a training block).** The cohort is 19.2 ± 0.7 y, so mean − 2·SD = 17.8 y. No range or minimum is stated, and "student cohort" does not establish adulthood. Resolve the age from the full text or supplements first. *Open question for D* if adulthood is resolved: the text gives only "end of each training week, Sunday". Figure 1 places the samples about 48 h after the Friday session. If D accepts the figure's day axis as reported timing, the individually measured WBC, CD4+ and CD8+ series (same 15 men, 4 identified weeks, W0 baseline) qualify as Support B. Otherwise the report is RETAIN_BACKGROUND, as both pilot stand-ins chose. The pooled urine proteome cannot give within-person evidence in either case. *Tempting:* FT03 because they are students; INCLUDE B without settling the age; EXCLUDE FT04.

**X04 · INCLUDE_A · age: adults_confirmed (25.5 ± 2.7 → 20.1 y) · E5: yes.** There are two post-cessation samples: the 40-min end-of-exercise sample and the 1 h post sample. During-exercise samples are ancillary. FT05 applies only when every sample is taken during exercise. A short "Did you know?" format with original data is coded by its review status and is not FT01 (PRE-004 C18). The record was layer U at triage (title only) and reaches full text only if B and C place it in V. *Tempting:* FT05; FT01 for the short format.

**X05 · INCLUDE_A · age: adults_confirmed · E5: yes (see X13).** "Two days after" converts to 48 h (bin 24–72 h, flag DAY_LEVEL_TIMING). A single late sample is a valid post-cessation sample. If the only sample had fallen after 72 h, the report would still be eligible, with that sample tagged as outside-window recovery: no code refers to the window. *Tempting:* FT05 because there is no immediate sample; excluding as "outside 0–72 h".

**X06 · INCLUDE_A · age: adults_confirmed (29 ± 5 → 19 y) · E5: yes (omics_discovery).** An immediate-only sample is eligible as a response; do not call it recovery. A sample size of 4 is not an exclusion reason. At extraction, n_recruited is 4. n_analysed is either 3 or 2, depending on whether the §2.2 and §2.3 exclusions refer to the same runner, so write UNCLEAR with a note; cells do not replace donors. *Tempting:* FT05 or "no recovery sample"; excluding for small n; recording the 4,211 cells as n.

**X07 · INCLUDE_A · FT06 does not apply.** A pre-bout baseline on the day before the race is enough; a non-exercise control is not required. The LRTI comparison between groups is an extra contrast, not a substitute for the baseline. *Tempting:* FT06 because there is no control group or the baseline is "not same-day".

**X08 · INCLUDE_A (Core A only; not B) · age: see X11.** Each participant does one exercise bout and one rest trial. B needs the same marker after **at least two identified exercise bouts** in the same people. The crossover is a single bout with a matched non-exercise comparator, and under v1.2 a crossover is not repeated monitoring. The null EX–CON result has no bearing on eligibility. *Tempting:* INCLUDE_A_AND_B, counting the two trials as two bouts; E5 = repeated_monitoring.

**X09 · Core-eligible on immune link; report disposition AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION, comment "frontier preprint".** FT07 does not apply. The immune link rests on direct measures: plasma chemokines measured on the targeted proteomic panel (CXCL16, CCL16, CXCL17; recognised immune mediators) and the whole-blood transcriptome (immune-cell omics), analysed in an explicit immune section. It does not rest on enrichment alone. Pathway terms and deconvolved cell proportions are charted as computational annotation, not as direct counts or function. A post-hoc pathway hit in a muscle or metabolic study **with no** direct immune measure would be FT07. The report is a bioRxiv preprint, so it goes to the frontier register; the age (41 ± 15 y) is unresolved, and should be resolved from the cited MoTrPAC protocol report. *Tempting:* FT07, reading "biochemical responses" as non-immune; INCLUDE_A as core evidence.

**X10 · AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION · age: mean_minus_2SD_below_18_unresolved (25 ± 7 → 11 y) · FT08 does not apply.** Only the placebo arm is analysed, and it has pre-bout baselines. The exercise response is therefore not mixed with the colostrum effect, and vitamin D status is a grouping, not an intervention. The age rule blocks inclusion until the parent trial report (ref. 26, a linked report of the same cohort) gives a range or minimum. *Tempting:* FT08 because the cohort came from "supplement trials"; INCLUDE_A without checking the age; FT03.

**X11 · INCLUDE_A for the report · age: adults_confirmed (Study 1: 46 − 18 = 28 y; 43 − 16 = 27 y).** Study 1 is a pre and immediately-post marathon (an identifiable competition) with salivary SIgA, in adults. The Study 2 cohort gives 22 − 6 = 16 y (men 17 y, women 15 y) and no range. It stays awaiting classification at cohort level and is not charted until resolved. Note this in the comment so that D tracks the cohort. *Tempting:* AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION for the whole report; FT03 for Study 2.

**X12 · EXCLUDE · FT03 · age: under_18_included.** The report states participants were 13–17 y, so the age rule's AWAITING route does not apply. FT03 comes before every other dimension, so the design otherwise fitting Core A does not matter. In the formal flow this record was already excluded at stage 1 (TI03). *Tempting:* INCLUDE_A for the design; AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION by misapplying the mean − 2·SD rule.

**X13 · E5 confirmed: yes for both (outcome_linkage).** R59 defines LRTI clinically, measures it over 2 weeks and compares post-race lysozyme by LRTI status. R56 defines RTI by questionnaire and tests the mucosal response against RTI by logistic regression. A tested association counts even when it is null (OR 1.00); background framing would not. Chart the outcome classes as reported: R59 applies a clinical case definition to self-reported symptoms, and R56 uses self-reported symptoms; neither is pathogen-confirmed. *Tempting:* "no" for R56 because the association was null; recording metric_validation.

**X14 · R46: yes (omics_discovery; targeted proteomic profiling of immune proteins).** The LOO random forest predicts a marker's own later concentration within the same 12 people. It is internal model checking: chart it under independent validation as internal, not as metric validation of an outcome. **R12: unclear (discuss).** The aims tie the metabolomics to immune modulation, but the separation reported is by vitamin D status, and the immune-relevant candidates (inflammatory lipid mediators) need checking in the results tables. R12's immune link does not depend on this, because neutrophil function and leukocyte counts are measured directly. *Tempting:* R46 = metric_validation; R12 = yes by default.

**X15 · DUPLICATE_REPORT: the R01 preprint only (linked to the Sci Adv version, which is the core report).** The differing title and date are version changes, not a separate study. R12 and its parent trial report the same cohort but different analyses, so they are **linked reports**: each is screened and the cohort is counted once. The two R03 PDFs are one report held twice; they make one record and no disposition is needed. *Tempting:* DUPLICATE_REPORT for R12's parent trial, which loses data; screening the R01 preprint as a second core report.
