# Stage-1 (title) AI stand-in screening — protocol v3.1, PRE-005 two-stage screening, AI-assisted design (2026-10-05/06)

Inputs: the frozen deduplicated pool (`../records_master.csv`, 15,750 records; git-ignored) split into 79 batches of 200 titles (record_id, title, journal, year, source database). Rules given to every stand-in: `TI_rules.md` (closed list TI01–TI05 from the screening manual, carve-outs, trap list).

- `out_B/`: stand-in B (Sonnet; inclusion-oriented: advance unless a closed-list reason is evident; several agents used keyword scripts and then read every exclusion by hand).
- `out_C2/`: stand-in C (Sonnet; closed-list strict; every title read individually, keyword classifiers forbidden). This is the C pass used in the merge.
- `archive_haiku_C/`: an earlier, cheaper C pass (Haiku, keyword scripts) kept for the record only; not used in the merge.
- `out_O/`: blind third read (Opus) of every record on which B and C2 disagreed (2,032 records; 22 conflict batches in `conflict_batches/`).
- `ti_ai_merged.csv`: one row per record with both passes, the merged disposition (advance wins), the third read, the final AI disposition after the sports-journal safeguard (a TI04 "no exercise context" exclusion is overturned when the journal is a sports/exercise journal), and the human review scope.
- `ti_review_B.xlsx`, `ti_review_C.xlsx`: reviewer workbooks (titles only, no abstracts): sheets pilot_100_blind (100-record calibration sample, seed 20261005, AI columns hidden), conflicts (none left after the third read), sample_verify (seeded 10% of agreed AI exclusions plus 20% of third-read exclusions; AI columns hidden), ai_advanced and ai_excluded_unverified (information).
- `ti_ai_summary.json`: counts. Result: 15,750 → 7,606 advance to stage 2 (8,144 excluded at title: TI02 3,381; TI04 3,138; TI05 841; TI03 443; TI01 341).

Human decisions (B, C) are entered in the workbooks and merged by D; AI dispositions are recommendations and are reported as such. Generated by `scripts/ti_ai_merge.py`.
