# Stage-1 (title) AI stand-in screening — protocol v3.1, PRE-005 two-stage screening, AI-assisted design (2026-10-05/06)

Inputs: the frozen deduplicated pool (`../records_master.csv`, 15,750 records; git-ignored) split into 79 batches of 200 titles (record_id, title, journal, year, source database). Rules given to every stand-in: `TI_rules.md` (closed list TI01–TI05 from the screening manual, carve-outs, trap list).

- `out_B/`: stand-in B (Sonnet; inclusion-oriented: advance unless a closed-list reason is evident; several agents used keyword scripts and then read every exclusion by hand).
- `out_C2/`: stand-in C (Sonnet; closed-list strict; every title read individually, keyword classifiers forbidden). This is the C pass used in the merge.
- `archive_haiku_C/`: an earlier, cheaper C pass (Haiku, keyword scripts) kept for the record only; not used in the merge.
- `out_O/`: blind third read (Opus) of every record on which B and C2 disagreed (2,032 records; 22 conflict batches in `conflict_batches/`).
- `ti_ai_merged.csv`: one row per record with both passes, the merged disposition (advance wins), the third read, the final AI disposition after the sports-journal safeguard (a TI04 "no exercise context" exclusion is overturned when the journal is a sports/exercise journal), and the human review scope.
- `ti_review_B.xlsx`, `ti_review_C.xlsx`: reviewer workbooks (titles only, no abstracts): sheets pilot_100_blind (100-record calibration sample, seed 20261005, AI columns hidden), conflicts (none left after the third read), sample_verify (seeded 10% of agreed AI exclusions plus 20% of third-read exclusions; AI columns hidden), ai_advanced and ai_excluded_unverified (information).
- `ti_ai_summary.json`: counts. Result: 15,750 → 7,606 advance to stage 2 (8,144 excluded at title: TI02 3,381; TI04 3,138; TI05 841; TI03 443; TI01 341).

Human decisions (B, C) are entered in the workbooks and merged by D; AI dispositions are recommendations and are reported as such. Generated by `scripts/ti_ai_merge.py`.

## PRE-007 rule-4 consequence (2026-10-09)

Human verification of the 978-record exclusion sample and the 100-record blind pilot (B and C, each pre-filled per PRE-008 choice 8, then independently verified; 177 disagreements adjudicated by D from the title, `stage1_human_disagreements_for_D.xlsx`) found confirmed advances at 27 % of sampled TI04 and 21 % of sampled TI05 exclusions, ≤2.4 % for TI01–TI03 (`stage1_sample_verification_2026-10-09.md`). The divergence was a rule-reading difference, written down as the v1.1 clarification at the end of `TI_rules.md` and adopted by A. Consequence applied in `ti_ai_merged.csv` (new columns `ai_final_prev`, `ai_final_code_prev`, `stage1_adjustment`):

- `HUMAN_SAMPLE_ADVANCE_B_C_AGREED` / `HUMAN_SAMPLE_ADVANCE_D_ADJUDICATED`: 142 sampled or pilot records the AI had excluded, now ADVANCE_TO_ABSTRACT by human decision of record.
- `RESCREEN_V1_1_ADVANCE` / `RESCREEN_V1_1_EXCLUDE`: the 3,439 unsampled TI04/TI05 exclusions re-screened by one inclusion-oriented stand-in (GPT-6 Sol, effort medium, TI_rules v1.1; `rescreen_2026-10-09/`, run 2 of record): 919 re-advanced, 2,520 retained (codes may be reassigned).
- `HUMAN_PILOT_TITLE_EXCLUDE_SUPERSEDED_BY_STAGE2`: 3 pilot records B and C excluded at title (TI05) had already been screened at stage 2 on their abstracts (2 EXCLUDE_TA, 1 ADVANCE → layer M); the abstract-level decision stands.

Stage-1 result after the consequence: 8,667 advance / 7,083 exclude (TI02 3,563; TI04 2,169; TI05 552; TI03 451; TI01 348 — counts in `ti_ai_summary.json` → `update_2026-10-09`). The 1,061 new entrants (`rescreen_2026-10-09/stage2_new_entrants.csv`) go through stage 2 with the same two stand-in families as batches 235/236 (B side GPT-6 Sol, C side GLM-5.3 thinking on), third reads of conflicts, then validation-readiness triage; new V-layer records are added to B's and C's verification workbooks as an addendum. TI01–TI03 exclusions outside the sample are not re-screened (confirmed-advance rates within the PRE-007 tolerance; reported as AI exclusions with 10 % sample verification).
