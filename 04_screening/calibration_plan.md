# Screening calibration plan

**Protocol:** v3.1 (amendments PRE-004 and PRE-005), 2026-10-05 · **Status:** the initial 50-record title/abstract pilot (round 1) was run on 2026-10-05 and accepted by A as calibration (raw agreement 1.00; AI hint visible, recorded as a limitation; `screening_log_template.json` → `calibration.result_2026-10-05`). The 100-record title pilot (stage 1) and the 25-record title/abstract re-calibration under v3.1 (stage 2) have not been run.

**Timing (v3 contract §4).** Calibration takes place **after** the v3.0 archive release (GitHub Release archived by Zenodo with a version DOI) and before the formal database search (PubMed, Web of Science Core Collection and Scopus under amendment PRE-006). Record the release tag, the commit SHA and the version DOI as the manual version in the calibration record (`screening_log_template.json` → `calibration.protocol_release_tag`, `protocol_release_commit`, `protocol_version_doi`). If calibration changes any rule, revise the manual and the templates, then release v3.1 with justification (new version DOI under the same concept DOI) before the formal search. The machine-readable form of the pass rule is `calibration.pass_rule` in `screening_log_template.json`; it must match this file.

## Reviewers and independence

Two human reviewers will independently screen every title (stage 1), every title/abstract retained at stage 1 (stage 2) and every retrieved full text (amendment PRE-005; `screening_manual.md` section 3A'). The project lead will record the two reviewer roles before screening starts. A third-person adjudicator role is reserved for unresolved conflicts, but the person's identity is pending and must not be fabricated. AI cannot replace either independent human reviewer or serve as the adjudicator. Reviewers use the same frozen manual and code list, and do not see each other's decisions until both have locked the relevant stage. Independence is implemented with Excel workbooks generated by `scripts/build_workbooks.py --only screening` from the JSON templates. Each reviewer receives their own copy, records decisions only in their own sheet, and locks it. The data manager records the SHA-256 of each locked workbook (`calibration.reviewer_workbook_sha256`; `workbook_workflow.locks` for formal stages) and commits the hashes to git **before** merging. Merging, agreement and kappa are then computed with `scripts/merge_screening.py merge --stage TA` (with `--calibration-ids` for the pilot sample), or with the workbook's merge_TA sheet.

## Initial pilot: 50 deduplicated records

1. After the v3.0 release, run the validated draft strategy to produce the draft search pool, then freeze and preserve the deduplicated draft record-ID pool before calibration. Save the pool manifest, its SHA-256 hash, draft-search/source provenance (not a claim that the formal search has run), and exact record IDs.
2. Sort unique record IDs lexicographically and draw 50 without replacement using Python 3's random.Random(20261002).sample(sorted(unique_record_ids), 50). The initial reproducible seed is **20261002** (kept unchanged in v3). Save the Python version, seed, sorted-pool manifest/hash, and drawn IDs in the calibration record. If fewer than 50 eligible unique IDs exist, do not silently reduce the sample: document the shortfall and obtain coordinator resolution before calling the calibration complete.
3. Both reviewers screen all 50 titles/abstracts independently using the controlled stage decision. For pilot agreement, calculate exact raw agreement on the binary primary disposition: **progress / do not exclude** (ADVANCE, AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION, or FRONTIER_PREPRINT) versus **EXCLUDE_TA**, across all 50 jointly assessed records. Report numerator, denominator, percentage, and Cohen's kappa descriptively. The threshold applies to raw agreement, not kappa.
4. Separately compare conceptual application of the manual, including: A/B timing (same marker in the same people after at least two bouts for B, Support B retained); position relative to the 0-72 h organising window (samples later than 72 h are tagged, not excluded); exercise intensity, duration and mode (recorded, never a threshold); age (adults >= 18 y, or a separable adult stratum in mixed-age cohorts); author open-window framing (never affects eligibility); and the health, immune-link, publication-status, translation, retrieval and mixed-intervention rules. Resolve and document **every conceptual disagreement**, even if both reviewers chose the same advance/exclude disposition. Update the manual or decision examples when discussion exposes ambiguity; preserve the change and rationale.
5. Calibration passes only if raw agreement is **at least 80%** and every conceptual disagreement has been resolved. This is a project rule, not a threshold attributed to JBI. Kappa is descriptive and has no pass threshold.
6. If raw agreement is below 80%, or conceptual disagreement remains unresolved, clarify the manual and run a fresh 50-record pilot. For reproducibility use Python random.Random(20261002 + round_number - 1) with round numbers beginning at 2 (round 2 seed: 20261003), sampling without replacement from the sorted frozen pool after excluding records used in earlier pilot rounds; if fewer than 50 unseen records remain, document the shortfall and expand the draft pool or obtain a recorded methodological resolution before proceeding; save each seed, sample IDs, manual version, and all statistics. Do not re-use the same exact sample as the sole repeat pilot. Repeat until both pass conditions are met; record actual outcomes without backfilling or altering the threshold.

Draft-pool pilot records enter the eventual formal screening pool only if retrieved by a documented formal search or citation-chasing route; calibration alone does not add them to its denominator. Following calibration, both reviewers independently complete stage-1 title decisions for the entire deduplicated formal-search pool and stage-2 title/abstract decisions for every record retained at stage 1, including any pilot records that belong to that pool, under the final clarified manual. Any pilot decision affected by a rule clarification is re-screened independently.

## Title pilot: 100 records (stage 1; v3.1, PRE-005)

1. After the v3.1 release and the formal searches, freeze the deduplicated union of the formal database exports (PubMed, Web of Science Core Collection, Scopus; amendment PRE-006) (records_master rows with a blank dedup_status) and save its manifest and SHA-256.
2. Draw 100 record IDs with Python random.Random(20261005).sample(sorted(unique_record_ids), 100). Save the Python version, seed, manifest/hash and drawn IDs in `screening_log_template.json` → `title_calibration`.
3. Both reviewers screen the 100 titles independently in the stage-1 workbook, seeing title, source, year, document type, DOI and PMID only, with no abstract and no AI hint, and applying the closed list in `screening_manual.md` section 3A'. Each reviewer records total minutes.
4. The pass rule is raw agreement of at least 80% on the binary title disposition, progress (ADVANCE_TO_ABSTRACT or AWAITING_CLASSIFICATION) versus EXCLUDE_TITLE, across all 100 jointly assessed records (`merge_screening.py merge --stage TI --calibration-ids`). Kappa is descriptive. Every disagreement, in disposition or closed-list code, is discussed and logged and the manual clarified where needed, but resolving disagreements is not a pass condition.
5. If raw agreement is below 80%, clarify the manual and draw a fresh 100 from unseen records with seed 20261005 + round_number - 1 (round 2: 20261006). Pilot records remain in the formal pool and are screened again at formal stage 1.

## Re-calibration of title/abstract screening under v3.1 (stage 2)

The initial 50-record round (seed 20261002) was screened under v3.0 codes. Because v3.1 changes the screening codes, a reduced round 2 precedes formal stage 2: 25 records drawn with Python random.Random(20261003).sample(sorted(stage1_retained_record_ids), 25) from the stage-1 retained set after abstract completion, excluding IDs used in earlier rounds. The pass rule of the initial pilot applies unchanged (raw agreement of at least 80% on the binary title/abstract disposition and every conceptual disagreement resolved; kappa descriptive), over 25 records (`merge_screening.py merge --stage TA --calibration-ids <ids> --planned-size 25`). The record is `calibration.recalibration_v3_1`. If the round fails, the repeat rule above applies (round 3 seed 20261004, 25 unseen records).

## Separate charting pilot

The 10-report charting pilot is purposive and uses the extraction materials in `05_extraction`. It also runs after the v3.0 archive release, and it is distinct from this random 50-record title/abstract pilot. Its reports may include excluded boundary cases. Do not substitute its sample size or agreement for screening calibration. Two humans independently extract the substantive fields; purely bibliographic entry may be completed by one and verified by the other. No pilot statistics or decisions are available yet.

## Full-text stage

Complete source-grounded full-text boundary exercises before formal full-text screening, documenting rule interpretations and unresolved questions. All reports advanced to full text are independently assessed by both reviewers using the fixed primary exclusion hierarchy. Preserve the original independent decisions, evidence locations, full-text retrieval state, discussion, final disposition, and any adjudicator referral. Full-text agreement is described; the initial 50-record title/abstract threshold is not silently reinterpreted as a full-text threshold. Discuss disagreements against the actual methods/results/supplement. Escalate only unresolved conflicts to the predesignated adjudicator.

## Records to retain

For each pilot round retain: protocol release tag, commit SHA and version DOI; frozen pool manifest and hash; seed and sampling algorithm; sample record IDs; reviewer role IDs; SHA-256 of each locked reviewer workbook and the commit that recorded them; date and manual version; independent decisions before discussion; raw agreement numerator/denominator; Cohen's kappa and method (including prevalence limitations or undefined kappa when no variation permits calculation); conceptual disagreement log; resolutions/manual edits; pass/fail against both prespecified conditions; and, if needed, the repeat-round seed and sample. Do not record a planned statistic as a result.

No formal search hits, deduplicated totals, pilot decisions, screening totals, exclusions, or PRISMA flow counts are available yet. They must be populated only after the corresponding work is actually completed.
