Precision definition (project operational framework, not regulator-approved score): appropriate interpretation for a defined person/subgroup, exercise context, sampling time and intended immune-monitoring use. Keep analytical reliability, temporal validity, biological/immune specificity, individualization, functional/clinical linkage, independent validation and feasibility separate. Group-average change/omics/AI alone do not demonstrate personalized utility. Categories are multidimensional, not a ladder. Missing = NR, inapplicable = NA, genuinely unclear = UNCLEAR; not reported is not proven absent.

RQ1: biomarkers/platforms/cell or fluid matrices and exercise/population contexts assessed after exercise.
RQ2: temporal response/recovery coverage and sensitivity to cell composition, hydration, sampling and other confounders.
RQ3: evidence for individual baselines/within-person variability/repeatability, response heterogeneity, subgroup or individualized models and validation.
RQ4: actual functional co-measurement versus demonstrated association, clinical outcomes and validated prediction/use; remaining development requirements.

Population: humans of any age/sex/training status; baseline generally healthy and no active infection. Distinct healthy strata of mixed cohorts eligible if extractable. Known clinical/metabolic disease treatment cohorts excluded main map; insulin resistance/metabolic-health ambiguity adjudicated explicitly, not automatically healthy. Supplement/feeding arms allowed if exercise effect separable (placebo/control arm or factorial contrast); otherwise excluded intervention not separable. Athletes/recreational/untrained, minors/adults/older age separately charted.

Core A: identifiable acute bout/competition (endurance/resistance/interval/mixed), with >=1 post-cessation sample AND pre-bout baseline or suitable nonexercise/comparator condition. No <30-min exclusion, no hard 72h cap. Immediate-only studies show response, not longitudinal recovery. During-only excluded. No direction/significance requirement. Repeated acute challenges before/after training qualify A; extract training moderation separately.
Support B: repeated post-exercise monitoring across >=2 identified bouts in the same people, with known/recoverable sampling relation to those bouts, and a baseline/comparator enabling interpretation. Separate map from A. General weekly/seasonal resting monitoring with unknown last-bout timing, pure training pre/post snapshots with unknown exercise-to-sampling interval, and habitual-exercise cross-sectional studies are background only. This is a deliberate narrowing from the exploratory restart report. Xu 2020 pooled-urine training study is a boundary/background example under this rule unless full text clarifies timing; CIMA is background. A cohort can contribute to A and B with linked IDs; do not double count overall.

Do not require preregistration of the original study.
