1. FRAMING (decision: A)
   - Topic: precision biomarkers of the exercise-induced immune "open window".
   - The open window is an ORGANISING CONSTRUCT: it defines the post-exercise time window, the sampling time points
     and the marker families to be charted. It is NOT a hypothesis the review adjudicates. No sentence may claim the
     review tests whether the open window exists; author interpretations are charted as a tag, as in v2.
   - Deliverable: per-marker validation-readiness map across conventional immune indices and omics candidates,
     using the v2 seven precision-evidence domains unchanged (analytical reliability, temporal validity, immune
     specificity, individualisation, functional/clinical association, independent + use validation, feasibility).
   - Introduction must position against: Peake 2017 (rationale anchor: "except for salivary IgA, clear and consistent
     markers of this immunodepression remain elusive"), Campbell & Turner 2018, Simpson 2020, Reitzner & Brodin 2026,
     Xie 2026 and Shi 2025 (both Front Immunol), Mănescu 2026, Davison 2025, plus existing refs 4-7. No "first review"
     or "no competing review" claim.

2. ELIGIBILITY (decisions: B, C for exposure; A + immunology content expert for window; A for population and B)
   2.1 Exposure threshold: NONE. Any identifiable single bout qualifies for Core A. Intensity, duration and mode are
       extraction fields and evidence-map strata, never eligibility filters. Rationale: open-window literature
       defines "prolonged/intense" inconsistently; thresholds would discard comparator evidence.
   2.2 Organising time window: 0-72 h after cessation of the identifiable bout. Immediate (0 h) samples are kept.
       Samples beyond 72 h are extracted as usual and tagged "outside-window recovery" (they document recovery
       endpoints). No new exclusion code is created; the FT01-FT08 hierarchy is unchanged.
   2.3 Population: adults (>= 18 y), generally healthy people and athletes. Older adults are included and flagged as a
       subgroup. Children and adolescents (< 18 y) are excluded (distinguishes this review from OSF osf.io/4hsdr).
   2.4 Support B is RETAINED as the cross-bout open-window monitoring layer: same person, same marker, >= 2 identified
       bouts, explicit timing, baseline/suitable comparator; deduplicated against Core A by cohort ID and presented
       separately. It is the main evidence source for the individualisation and repeatability domains.
   2.5 Immune-linkage rule, omics rule, preprint register, background-only status of resting cross-sectional training
       studies: unchanged from v2.

3. SEARCH (consequence of 1-2)
   - The strategy must NOT depend on the phrase "open window" (18 PubMed title/abstract hits 2018-2026). Search by
     construct: exercise x immune and exercise x omics routes as in v2, with eligibility and the 0-72 h window applied
     at screening. "Prespecified recovery windows" (Trybulski 2026) is a citable precedent for window-based charting.
   - Seed list must include open-window-centred originals and boundary cases (immediate-only, >72 h-only, youth, resting).

9. AMENDMENT PRE-003 (2026-10-04) — T-BLOCK REQUIRED; PRESS CONDITIONS (decision: A)
   9.1 Supersedes §3 ("construct x routes with ... the 0-72 h window applied at screening", insofar as it implies no
       timing concept in the query) and ruling 8.3a ("The post-exercise/recovery T-block stays diagnostic only").
       New rule: the post-exercise/acute-bout T-block is a REQUIRED concept: E AND (I OR O) AND T. T expresses that
       measurements follow an identifiable bout; it does not encode the 0-72 h organising window, which together with
       all other eligibility rules is still applied at screening. "open window"-type terms remain OR supplements or
       citation-chasing routes only, never AND terms (rest of 8.3a unchanged).
   9.2 Evidence (PubMed parser validation 2026-10-04; 03_search/platform_validation_2026-10-04.md, Addendum 2):
       v0.6 construct-at-screening union E AND (I OR O) = 255,019 records (two-reviewer title/abstract screening
       ~8,500 person-hours at 1 min/record/reviewer, before the other four databases; assumed five-database raw
       multiplier x1.5-2.5 before dedup). Requiring T with the proximity fix ("after marathon"[tiab:~1]) = 15,930
       records (~15,900), 10/10 positive PubMed seeds retrieved. Limits: evidence base = 10 positive PubMed seeds;
       the proximity fix was designed post hoc after the POS-C1 miss; known blind spot = reports that give timing
       only numerically (e.g. "at 1 h and 24 h"), untested; PubMed only.
   9.3 Trade-off disclosed in protocol E as recall given up for feasibility. Mitigations (all required):
       a. Positive seed set expanded to >= 30 reports, including >= 5 numeric-only timing reports; re-tested on all
          platforms in the pilot before formal searching.
       b. Backward and forward citation chasing of all included reports and of the five citation-chasing reviews
          (Peake 2017, Campbell & Turner 2018, Simpson 2020, Shi 2025, Xie 2026).
       c. T-free sensitivity run during the pilot: a random sample of records retrieved by E AND (I OR O) but not by
          T is screened; if it or the seed re-test shows unacceptable loss, T is revised as a further amendment
          before formal searching.
   9.4 PRESS accepted with conditions (A, 2026-10-04):
       a. NO infection-outcome terms in the strategy (no URTI/"upper respiratory"/"respiratory infection*" etc.).
          Eligibility runs through the immune-link rule; infection outcomes are charted when present.
       b. train* retained until the pilot sample test of its marginal yield (PRESS 4.8).
       c. Protocol E must no longer state that the strategy covers infection terms.
   9.5 Recorded as PRE-003 in amendments.json; included in the v3.0 release (pre-archive, pre-pilot,
       pre-formal-search). 03_search is revised to v0.7 by D under this amendment.
